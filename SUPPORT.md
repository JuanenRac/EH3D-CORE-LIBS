# Support

* **Questions and ideas:** open an issue with the form that fits.
* **A file that is read wrongly:** attach it (or the smallest one that shows the problem), say which reader you used and what you expected, and paste the diagnostics the reader returned.
* **What a reader promises:** see `docs/READERS.md` first.

This is a one-person project and the suite is at its first steps: answers can take a few days.
